Privacy Policy

RouteConnect • Manjhepur – Amauli – Jahanabad – Kanpur • Last updated: 19 November 2026 (version 2026-11)

1. What RouteConnect is

RouteConnect is a website that shows verified captains (vehicle drivers) who run on the Manjhepur–Kanpur corridor, so passengers can see timings and contact them by Call or WhatsApp. RouteConnect does not run vehicles and does not take payment for rides.

2. What we collect

WhoDataWhy
PassengersName and Gmail address (from Google sign-in), mobile number you type in, a random device ID stored in your browser, the time you accepted this policyTo identify who contacts a captain, for safety and to block misuse
PassengersA record each time you tap Call or WhatsApp for a captain: time, captain, vehicle, and your details aboveSafety record and dispute handling

We do not collect your location, your Google password, call recordings or the content of WhatsApp chats.

Your mobile number is typed by you and is not verified by an OTP. It cannot be changed later by you. If you made a mistake, contact us (section 8).

3. What other people can see

  • Your review shows your name, stars, tags, comment and date to everyone on the website.
  • Captains do not receive your Gmail or mobile number from RouteConnect. If you call or message a captain, they will see your phone or WhatsApp number as in any normal call or chat.
  • Captain trip details (name, vehicle, phone number, photo, route and timing) are public so that passengers can contact them.

4. Who we may share data with

  • Police and courts: only on a lawful request, or where needed to prevent or investigate a crime or threat to safety. This can include your name, Gmail, mobile number, device ID and contact records.
  • Service providers: Google (sign-in) and Firebase / Google Cloud (database and hosting). They process data on our behalf.
  • We do not sell your data and do not show advertisements based on it.

5. How long we keep it

  • Call / WhatsApp contact records: up to 12 months, then they are deleted.
  • Passenger account details: until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Reviews: until you or the admin remove them. Removed reviews are kept in a private archive for audit.
  • Blocked accounts and blocked devices: while the block remains, so that it keeps working.
  • Captain trips: removed automatically after the trip ends or expires.
  • On your own phone, a copy of your recent contact history is kept for 25 days and shown in “My Activity”.

6. Device ID

We store a random ID in your browser and attach it to your records. If an account is blocked for misuse, the devices it used can also be blocked. The ID is not linked to your name outside RouteConnect. Clearing your browser data creates a new ID.

7. Your choices and rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or withdraw your consent. If you withdraw consent or delete your account, you will not be able to use Call / WhatsApp booking or post reviews. We may keep records we are legally required to keep, or that are needed for an open safety complaint.

8. Contact and complaints

For any request or complaint about your data, write to shivanimakandipur@gmail.com with the Gmail you used on RouteConnect. We will reply as soon as we can.

9. Children

RouteConnect is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please use it only with a parent or guardian.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and ask you to accept it again when you sign in.
